## ⚡️ The Specifics of Playing Aviator

The original Aviator game, released in 2017, is built around a very simple idea. A player places a bet, waits for the round to begin, and then watches a small plane take off.

As soon as the countdown finishes, the plane starts flying upward. At the same time, the multiplier begins to grow. The higher the plane goes, the bigger the possible payout becomes.

However, this is where the main risk appears. The plane can crash at any moment. If a player does not cash out before the crash, the bet is lost.

That is why timing is the most important part of Aviator. A player has to decide when to leave the round and collect the winnings. Some users prefer an aggressive strategy and wait for a higher multiplier, hoping to receive a much larger reward.

Other players choose a more careful approach. They cash out during the first seconds of the round and accept smaller winnings in exchange for a lower level of risk.
